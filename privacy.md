Flynx Privacy Policy
Last updated: November 8, 2026

Flynx is a browser extension that keeps Netflix playback in sync between people in the same private room. Flynx is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Netflix.

What we process
To make syncing work, the extension sends the following to our server: the room name or code you enter, the Netflix video ID of the title you are watching, and playback events (play, pause, seek) with the current timestamp. This information is passed to the other people in your room.

What we do not collect
We do not collect your name, email, Netflix account details, viewing history, payment information, or any personal information. We do not read any page content beyond the video player state. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Storage and retention
Room data exists only in server memory while someone is connected and is deleted when the last person leaves the room. We do not store it in a database or keep logs of it.

Sharing
We do not sell or transfer your data to third parties. Data is shared only with other users in the same room, and only to provide the sync feature. We do not use it for any other purpose, including advertising, credit, or lending decisions.

Hosting
Our server is hosted on Render, which may process network connection data (such as IP addresses) as part of normal hosting operations. We do not access or store this.

Security
All communication uses encrypted WebSocket connections (wss://). Anyone who knows a room name can join it, so use a hard-to-guess code for private sessions.

Children
Flynx is not directed at children under 13.

Changes
If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
